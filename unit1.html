Unit 1: What's he like?

学习描述人物性格特征的形容词，并询问他人性格特点。

第一部分：主课文对话

在教室里，老师向学生们介绍新同学和老师。

T
老师: "This is Amy. She's quiet. She's very hard-working."
T
老师: "That is Wu Binbin. He's very clever. He's polite, too."
O
Oliver: "Hello. My name is Oliver."
A
Amy: "Hi."
W
Wu Binbin: "Hello."

第二部分：What's he like? (他/她是什么样的人？)

学生们正在讨论新来的老师。

A
学生A: "We have a new PE teacher. He's a good football player."
B
学生B: "Cool!"
A
学生A: "Ms Wang will be our new Chinese teacher."
B
学生B: "What's she like?"
A
学生A: "She's very kind."
B
学生B: "Is she strict?"
A
学生A: "Yes, sometimes."

第三部分：Let's try & Let's talk

这是一个听力练习，请根据录音判断对话内容。下面是对话文本：

W
Wu Binbin: "Do you know Mr Young?"
O
Oliver: "No, I don't. Who is he?"
W
Wu Binbin: "He's our music teacher."
O
Oliver: "Is he young?"
W
Wu Binbin: "No, he isn't. He's old."
O
Oliver: "Is he funny?"
W
Wu Binbin: "Yes, he is."
O
Oliver: "Great! I like funny teachers."

第四部分：Let's learn

点击卡片，学习描述人物性格的形容词。

old 年老的 🔊
young 年轻的 🔊
funny 有趣的 🔊
kind 和蔼的 🔊
strict 严格的 🔊

第五部分：Let's spell

字母 y 在单词结尾时，通常发 /i/ 的音。选择下面单词中发这个音的单词。

1. happy

2. my

3. family

第六部分：Let's try & Let's talk (Part B)

听听陈洁和 John 的对话，他们的新老师是什么样的？

J
John: "Really? What's she like?"

第七部分：Let's learn (Part B)

学习更多描述性格的词汇。

polite 有礼貌的 🔊
hard-working 勤奋的 🔊
helpful 乐于助人的 🔊
clever 聪明的 🔊
shy 害羞的 🔊

第八部分：Read and write

认识 Robin！

日期： Sept. 1st, Tuesday

"I have a robot! His name is Robin. My grandpa made him. Robin is short but strong. He is really clever. He can speak Chinese and English.

He is hard-working. He is very helpful at home. He is strict, too. He makes me finish my homework!"

What is Robin like? (Read and tick or cross)

1. tall

2. short

3. thin

4. strong

5. old

6. helpful

7. hard-working

8. strict

9. clever

Design a robot of your own

画一画、写一写你自己的机器人。

第九部分：Let's check

这是一个综合听力练习，请在老师的指导下完成。

Listen and circle.

1. Miss Chen is a funny/science teacher.

2. Mr Grey is a music/Chinese teacher.

Listen again and answer.

1. "What is Miss Chen like?"

2. "What is Mr Grey like?"

第十部分：Let's wrap it up

复习人称代词和be动词的正确搭配。

  • you, we, they + are
  • I + am
  • it, he, she + is

练习 (Choose and write):

1. I ___ helpful!

2. He ___ funny.

3. She ___ kind.

4. It ___ small.

5. They ___ old.

6. We ___ strong!

第十一部分：Story time

一个关于新体育老师的有趣故事。

1

Zip: "Our new PE teacher will come today!"
Zoom: "Really? What's he like?"

2

Zip: "I only know he's a good football player."
Zoom: "He must be tall and strong!"

3

Zip: "Shoot!"
Zoom: "Oh, no! I missed!"

4

Rabbit: "Can I join you?"
Zoom: "You're very thin and so short. Can you play football?"
Rabbit: "Sure. Let me show you."

5

Rabbit scores nine goals.
Rabbit: "Ten to three! We win!"

6

Zoom: "You're great!"
Dog: "Our new PE teacher is a great football player, too."
Rabbit: "I am your new PE teacher!"
