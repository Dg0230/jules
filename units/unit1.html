Unit 1 School

单元主题： 学校生活和学习用品

学生A: "I have a pencil." (我有一支铅笔。)

学生B: "I have a ruler." (我有一把尺子。)

课堂学习场景

Miss Wu (老师): "I have a book." (我有一本书。)

Lesson 1: 词汇学习

A: Look, listen and chant 🎵

图片单词 (Word)音标 (Phonetics)中文含义 (Meaning)
📖book[bʊk]书
📏ruler[ˈruːlər]尺子
✏️pencil[ˈpensl]铅笔
🎒schoolbag[ˈskuːlbæɡ]书包
👩‍🏫teacher[ˈtiːtʃər]老师

B: Listen and do 🎧

Miss Wu: "Stand up, Joy. Show me your pencil. Sit down, please." (乔伊，站起来。给我看看你的铅笔。请坐下。)

课堂用语重点: Stand up, Show me, Sit down, Please.

Lesson 2: 句型练习

A: Look, listen and repeat 🔄

Andy: "I have a ruler." (我有一把尺子。)

Yaoyao: "I have a pencil." (我有一支铅笔。)

B: Let's play 🎮

Andy: "I have a schoolbag." (我有一个书包。)

Joy: "I have a book." (我有一本书。)

Lesson 3: 游戏学习

A: Let's play 🎮 (闭眼猜物游戏)

游戏规则: 闭上眼睛 (Close your eyes)，然后睁开眼睛猜什么不见了 (Open your eyes. What's missing?)

Miss Wu: "Open your eyes. What's missing?" (睁开眼睛。什么不见了？)

学生们: "Pencil." "Pencil." "Pencil." (铅笔。)

B: Let's sing 🎵

Time to sing a song to practice our new words!
