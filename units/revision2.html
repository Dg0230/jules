热带岛屿学习之旅 (Tropical Island Learning Journey)

活动： 在热带岛屿上进行一次全面的复习之旅，涵盖所有单元的知识。

复习路径游戏 (Review Path Game)

Follow the path and answer questions about all the topics we've learned!

练习句型 (Practice Sentences):

  • "Do you like...?" (e.g., apples)
  • "What colour is it?"
  • "How many are there?"
  • "I have a..."
  • "This is my..."
  • "What's this?"

复习词汇 (Review Vocabulary):

  • Fruits: apple, pear, banana, orange
  • Colours: red, green, blue, yellow, black
  • Numbers: one-ten
  • Animals: dog, cat, tiger, monkey, bird
  • Face: eye, ear, nose, mouth
  • School: book, pencil, ruler, schoolbag
