航海探险棋盘游戏 (Sailing Adventure Board Game)

活动： 沿着航海路线，复习第一至第三单元的知识。

复习内容 (Review Content):

  • Unit 1 (School): book, ruler, pencil, schoolbag, teacher
  • Unit 2 (Face): eye, ear, nose, mouth
  • Unit 3 (Animals): dog, cat, bird, monkey, tiger

练习句型 (Practice Sentences):

  • "I have a..."
  • "This is my..."
  • "What's this? It's a..."

安全岛探险 (Safety Island Adventure)

活动： 在安全岛的不同区域进行主题复习。

身体部位复习区 (Body Parts Zone)

练习句型: "This is my mouth." "This is my eye."

动物世界探索区 (Animal World Zone)

练习句型: "What's this?" "It's a monkey."
