Unit 2: My week

学习一周七天、学校课程以及如何讨论你的时间安排。

第一部分：开篇

让我们通过一段对话来开始本周的学习！

A
问: What do you have on Fridays?
B
答: I have a PE class.
A
问: Do you often play sports?
B
答: No, I don't. I don't like sports.

第二部分：Let's try & Let's talk

J
John: Grandpa! Look at my picture.
G
Grandfather: Great! What do you have on Thursdays?
J
John: I have maths, English and music.
G
Grandfather: Oh, I love music! Who's your music teacher?
J
John: Mr Young.
G
Grandfather: Is he strict?
J
John: No. He's funny. What do you do on Thursdays, Grandpa?
G
Grandfather: Oh, I have a cooking class with your grandma!
J
John: Haha!

第四部分：Let's spell

听句子，并填入单词中缺失的字母 ee 或 ea。

1. I like to r d by the sea.
2. Have some t and b f.
3. M t the baby.

第五部分：Let's try & Let's talk (Part B)

听一段公园里的对话，注意他们谈论的周末活动。

ZP
张鹏: Hi, Sarah. What's that?
S
Sarah: It's a storybook.
ZP
张鹏: Do you often read books in this park?
S
Sarah: No, I don't. Do you often play football here?
ZP
张鹏: Yes, I do. I like this park very much.
S
Sarah: Me too.

第六部分：Let's learn (Part B)

学习描述周末活动的常用短语。

wash my clothes 洗衣服 🔊
watch TV 看电视 🔊
do homework 做作业 🔊
read books 读书 🔊
play football 踢足球 🔊

第七部分：Read and write

根据故事内容，判断下列句子是否正确。

1. Wu Binbin often watches TV on the weekend.

2. Wu Binbin often reads books on the weekend.

3. Wu Binbin likes sports.

第八部分：Let's check

Listen and number (听力练习)

这是一个听力练习，请在老师的指导下完成。

Read and circle (阅读并圈出)

1. Do you read books on the weekend?

2. Do you watch TV on the weekend?

3. Do you wash your clothes on the weekend?

第九部分：Let's wrap it up

将右边的词尾拖到左边正确的词根上，组成一个完整的单词！

第十部分：Story time

阅读下面的小故事，学习如何合理规划购物。

1

Zoom: "It's a new school year. I need to do some shopping."
Zip: "I'll help."

2

Zip: "We have English on Mondays and Wednesdays."
Zoom: "I need some notebooks and pencils."

3

Zip: "We have maths on Tuesdays."
Zoom: "I need a ruler."

4

Zip: "On Thursdays, we have art."
Zoom: "I need a box of crayons."

5

Zip: "We have PE on Fridays."
Zoom: "I want a new football, please. We can play with it on the weekend."

6

Zip: "I'm sorry, Zoom. We don't have enough money. I'll put back the football."
Zoom: "Oh, no!"
